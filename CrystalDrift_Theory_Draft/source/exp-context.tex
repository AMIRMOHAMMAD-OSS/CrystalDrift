\paragraph{Inputs and reference protocols.}
A useful comparator must answer the same conditional question. DiffCSP, FlowMM, CrystalFlow and uFlowCSP accept the elements and their cell counts in their composition-conditioned configurations \cite{diffcsp,flowmm,crystalflow,uflow}. Lang2Str instead predicts a space group from the composition, generates a text description and then decodes the lattice and sites with a conditioned flow. Its MP-20 Table~4 reports 63.92\% recovery under its ordinary protocol and 78.33\% for the separately labeled oracle \cite{lang2str}. Only the ordinary protocol is relevant to composition-only prediction. Its 10,000-draw de novo experiment reports 91.63\% chemical validity. Those published rates include the authors' language and geometry pipeline. MatterGen jointly generates periodic geometry and elements from a diffusion model trained on filtered MP and Alexandria records. Its discovery evaluation uses 1,024 samples, a competing-phase hull and a disorder-aware structural matcher \cite{mattergen}. Here the reported local raw and physical panels name their reference sets, force criteria and denominators separately.

The original drifting work evaluates image quality at a stated number of network calls, SymDrift reports conformer error and recall alongside inference time, and TokenDrift tests quality at fixed decoding budgets \cite{deng,symdrift,tokendrift}. We apply the same distinction to crystals: a generator may benefit from many cheap proposals even when it loses at a fixed number of completed proposals. The case-matched curves track the wall-clock completion time of each proposal. The end-to-end de novo timers additionally include the cost of choosing a composition.
