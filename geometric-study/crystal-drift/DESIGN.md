# A direct generator for periodic crystal structures

## Scope before fitting

Given the complete list of elements in the primitive cell, sample the cell metric and all fractional coordinates together in a single neural network evaluation. Train on the official WyckoffDiff-processed MP-20 training split and evaluate on the corresponding test split. The first cohort consists of one atom each of two distinct elements. The second includes binary primitive cells with two to eight atoms and repeated species. The binary training sets contain 512 and 3,630 structures respectively. Space group and Wyckoff labels are unavailable to the model. This is composition-conditioned structure generation.

Train with minibatch attraction to real crystals and repulsion from independent generated crystals. Use normalized log-cell coordinates, approximate chemistry neighborhoods, species-resolved Fourier coefficients and translation-invariant products of coefficients. Compare with a raw-fractional-coordinate version of the same loss on the two-atom cohort. The architecture and split stay fixed. On this cohort the complex phase difference at each of the three unit frequencies specifies the complete relative coordinate in the released lattice basis. A finite collection of descriptors can lose information for larger cells. Basis reduction and arbitrary changes of primitive basis are different operations.

Both models use 1,200 updates of 64 examples per optimizer step and three independent initialization seeds (11, 23, 37). Drifting also draws 64 reference examples per step. Compare a periodic conditional flow-matching model with 1, 4 or 16 Euler evaluations on the two-atom cohort and 1 or 16 on the larger cohort. Count training and sampling time and number of forward evaluations. Include retrieval from training examples using species information. Use an analytic torus seam and re-origining check to isolate coordinate convention effects. Do not tune using test outputs.

Primary target is lower held-out conditional energy score for an independent vector of physical descriptors. Also report short interatomic contacts, volume errors, diversity, and the difference from training retrieval. Use the same ground-truth structures and number of candidates for each comparison. One reference crystal per test entry supports ensemble scoring across targets but cannot establish physical polymorph probabilities. Treat superior accuracy, stability and novelty as open questions. Report the training loss as a mechanism check only. Report negative results for the raw-coordinate control or flow comparator where observed.

## One revision after the initial test results

The pooled chemistry kernel yielded a larger volume error than flow matching on both test cohorts. Examine one specific alternative, a separate empirical drift field for each exact cell composition. Freeze the network, optimizer, step count, initial seeds and temperatures. Use 16 randomly drawn formula occurrences per step, four queries per occurrence and separate banks of four observed and four simulated crystals with that formula. Draw observed references with replacement when needed. The same architecture can pool its learned parameters across formulas. The estimator's effective minibatch and within-row normalization also change, so this is a comparison of two training protocols.

Use the original test comparison as exploratory. With no tuning or selection using validation results, test the same paired contrast between the within-formula and pooled variants on the previously unused official validation structures. Report spread as well as fidelity because formulas with a single example provide a point-mass empirical conditional. Retain all initial comparisons. No further model variants are planned.
