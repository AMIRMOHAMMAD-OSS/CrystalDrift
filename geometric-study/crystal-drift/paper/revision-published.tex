\paragraph{Published MP-20 benchmarks.}
Table~3 of Dey et al.\ \cite{uflow} reports one-draw match rates of 51.49\%, 61.39\% and 62.02\% for DiffCSP, FlowMM and CrystalFlow \cite{diffcsp,flowmm,crystalflow}. The corresponding normalized RMSEs are 0.0631, 0.0566 and 0.0710. Their samplers used 1,000, 50--100 and 100 evaluations per draw, respectively. At twenty draws DiffCSP and CrystalFlow reached 77.93\% and 78.34\%, compared with 78.38\% for one-step uFlowCSP. The published times for 10,000 structures were 76.1, 13.2 and 6.5 minutes on an A800 for the first three methods and 0.39 minutes on an A100 for one-step uFlowCSP. We use the local timings in our cost comparison.
