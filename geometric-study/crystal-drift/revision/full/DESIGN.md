# Expanded frozen MP-20 recovery and WBM fits

The official 9,046 MP-20 test structures are evaluated without selection on chemistry or validity. Sort literal IDs by ascending SHA-256. For each of three original fits (11, 23, 37), every method receives the supplied elemental multiset and twenty ordered candidates. The 128 original hash-first targets retain the exact stored first five proposals. For every remaining target/draw, matched neural models receive the same Gaussian cell noise and uniform fractional noise. Retrieval uses only original MP-20 training geometries and donor IDs. Model weights, settings and training data are frozen. The first one and five candidates on all targets and the first twenty on the original 128 take priority over full twenty-candidate scoring.

For each candidate, record pymatgen 2024.4.13 normalized RMS by get_rms_dist, maximum-site success by fit, and the ratio of generated to deposited volume before scale normalization. Native-volume sensitivity requires an RMS match on the same candidate and a volume ratio in [1/1.10,1.10]. The RMS threshold, site, angle and lattice tolerances, primitive reduction and isotropic scaling match the previous study. For each budget report at-least-one recovery and mean best normalized RMS conditional on recovery. Use a paired nonparametric bootstrap over held-out IDs, averaging seed indicators on each target before resampling, 10,000 iterations. For RMS average the conditional mean separately inside each fitted seed, then give the three seed means equal weight, as in the original binary table. The fitted seeds remain fixed. Integrity checks compare stored overlapping rows, ID and species order, matched noise, native log volume and metric concordance against the public pymatgen methods. An unscored candidate is kept distinct from a miss.

WBM seeds 23 and 37 repeat the original seed-11 one-epoch paired drift/flow fit with one CPU thread, 12,819 updates, 205,091 unique training anchors and all previous settings. Use the previous 128 hash-first WBM test IDs and five proposals per model per seed. Combined reports link the original seed-11 records and the new fit and evaluator records in this directory. This experiment changes replication and evaluation coverage. The submitted manuscript tables and previous runs are retained.
