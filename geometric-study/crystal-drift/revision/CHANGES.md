# CrytalDrift revision, 20 November 2026

- The primary result now measures recovery under an actual serial deadline. Every proposal carries its completion timestamp. The fixed-draw comparison adds the remaining fifteen binary uFlowCSP samples per target, normalized displacement among recovered targets, and a timed 10,000-structure workload.
- The public MP-20 experiment spans all 9,046 test structures and retains the original 128-target full-chemistry prefix. The binary 128-target cohort has its own selection from the smaller binary split. The WBM analysis repeats the two trained arms at seeds 23 and 37 alongside seed 11.
- The comparison includes maximum-site displacement and agreement of native volume on the same candidate. Native-volume agreement removes the five-draw full-MP-20 advantage over one Euler step. Retrieval remains a strong control even on formula-disjoint WBM.
- The mathematical supplement specifies which symmetries are built in, where the deposited cell basis enters, when a finite bank has zero expected field, and how the eight-site collision extends to the 1,306-component representation. The new channel separates the two motifs. Across four feature sets and three seeds, the pair-only arm improved the independent geometry score, and the signed channel had no resolved recovery benefit.
- The de novo evaluation uses all 1,024 draws. The figures show the actual CIFs and retain the short Pd–Pd contact in a step-limited relaxation. Raw validity, coverage, energy change and physical force have separate denominators.
- The abstract, discussion, vector figures, citations, manuscript metadata and submission package reflect these experiments. The author, affiliation and model name are preserved.
