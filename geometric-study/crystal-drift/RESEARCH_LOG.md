# Research decisions and measured outcomes

The [November revision](revision/CHANGES.md) gives the full-split and matched-timing results. The stages below document the preceding experiments.


The project tested whether a detached attraction–repulsion field on periodic crystal features can train a fast generator of whole binary crystal cells. The primary scientific comparison uses the official MP-20 split. Each result below has saved per-seed or per-structure observations.

| Stage | Decision or observation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Prior art | One-step crystal prediction already exists in uFlowCSP. The proposed contribution is a compact model trained by geometric drifting and a measurement of its performance at short proposal budgets. | [Closest papers and distinctions](prior-art.md) |
| Initial design | Fix input chemistry, two nested binary cohorts, separate crystal features, local periodic flow and retrieval controls, and three seeds. Set an independent geometric energy score as the primary endpoint. | [Design record](DESIGN.md) |
| Geometry checks | Cross-powers recover the complete two-site relative displacement in the deposited basis. A finite multisite descriptor has no demonstrated completeness guarantee. | [Mathematics](MATHEMATICS.md) · [Checks](geometry-checks.json) |
| First test | Periodic features improved two-site geometric score relative to the raw chart. The pooled drift showed large absolute volume errors and trailed retrieval. | [Two-site comparisons](comparison/ab-paired-drift-minus.csv) · [Larger-cohort comparisons](comparison/multi-groups.csv) |
| Single model revision | Group references by exact cell formula. Freeze the architecture, temperatures and training budget before fitting. Keep the official test contrast exploratory and repeat it on the previously unused validation split. Validation score improved in both cohorts. | [Revision](DESIGN.md) · [Two-site validation](comparison/val-ab-paired-exact-minus.csv) · [Larger-cohort validation](comparison/val-multi-paired-exact-minus.csv) |
| Speed and external controls | Measure complete conditional proposals with materialized CPU output. On the 128-target multisite subset, ranking changes between five candidates per method and a common 5 ms serial generation budget. Replaying a stronger published one-step model exposed a large recovery gap. | [Timing data](speed-results/) · [Fixed-time outcomes](frontier-results/fixed-time.csv) · [Published-checkpoint replay](uflowcsp-results/README.md) |
| De novo generation | Freeze a fitted composition prior and a single model checkpoint. Generate 1,024 proposals. Send the first 32 to relaxation without filtering. The figure example rules use draw order and, for the appendix, training membership. | [Full manifest](de-novo/manifest.csv) · [Selection rationale](de-novo/figures/render-metadata.json) |
| Physical screening | Compare all 32 against held-out controls, available same-formula references, and elemental endpoints. Short contacts sometimes persisted or worsened after relaxation. Thermodynamic and dynamical stability and experimental synthesis remain open. | [Screen report](de-novo/relaxation/README.md) · [Elemental comparisons](de-novo/elemental-screen/candidate-gaps.csv) |
| Release checks | Regenerating the entire de novo stream reproduced the coordinates, manifest and exported CIFs byte for byte. The source archive compiles independently. Local CPU and laptop GPU results are identified by runtime. | [Sampling entry point](de_novo.py) · [Build and reproduction instructions](README.md) |
| Multielement extension | Trained matched drift and flow models with one complete anchor epoch on native MP-20 and formula-disjoint WBM. WBM drift recovered 39 of 128 targets within five proposals. One-step flow recovered 28, 16-step flow 47, retrieval 113. The WBM-trained comparison has one seed. | [Model and protocols](scaled/RUN.md) · [Outcomes](scaled/runs/) |
| Independent transfer | Prepared 607,683 training and 67,521 validation structures from Alex-MP-20. Tested the frozen MP-20 checkpoints on 128 Alexandria validation structures with reduced formulas absent from MP-20 training. Drift recovered 14.84% within five proposals, compared with 9.64% for one-step flow and 28.13% for retrieval. | [Alexandria release and reproduction](scaled/ALEX.md) |
| Fourier identifiability | Lifted a known cyclic homometric pair to two inequivalent decorated cells that coincide on all 70 trained features. A single collinear bispectrum separates them analytically. Its training effect remains unmeasured. | [Mathematical supplement](paper/supplement.tex) · [Exact and numerical check](scaled/harmonic_check.json) |
| De novo structural screen | Of the 1,024 raw binary proposals, 932 survived greedy uniqueness and found no match in either MP-20 or the eligible WBM reference set. The test covers these named libraries. | [Database screen](scaled/novelty/summary.json) |
