# Formula-only proposals from predicted Wyckoff templates

For each of 192 validation compositions, choose up to five distinct space-group and occupied-letter templates from labelled **training** records. Each donor has the requested primitive-cell atom count and the same list of per-species multiplicities. Order equal-multiplicity species by their Mendeleev number, then rank donors by the mean absolute change in Mendeleev number plus a two-unit substitution penalty per changed species. Remap the selected template's species to the query. The supplied-template model's existing train-only metric prior supplies the lattice and draws uniform free coordinates for each orbit. The angular-C network updates the expanded crystal once. The zero-call control uses the exact same seeds. Converting the output with the predicted group's centering basis restores the exact requested native cell composition. No donor fractional coordinates, query space group, Wyckoff letters, cell volume or coordinates enter these proposal functions.

The target IDs follow a salted hash of their IDs after excluding both published validation selection panels. The target's structure is first used for matching after all proposals are generated. The original formula-only angular-A seed-11 checkpoint runs on the same cases and draw budgets. The set of candidate training templates and their chemistry ranking provide additional information learned from the train split. This is a retrieval-assisted generator and transfers symbolic prototypes rather than complete candidate geometries; the released template prior still samples training-set cell shapes as part of its existing prior. The template-trained and formula-trained checkpoints differ. Their comparison cannot isolate a single parameter or architectural feature.

The file summary.json reports proposal-first StructureMatcher outcomes following the formula-only comparison. The supplied-template release used the reverse argument order, which is recorded independently per draw. A hit under the additional volume criterion also requires absolute log(volume ratio) no greater than log(1.1). Paired intervals resample the 192 target IDs at fixed checkpoints and random seeds. This pilot has no multi-seed or wall-clock estimate.

Run experiment.py and then strata.py under the pinned matcher environment, including PyTorch, NumPy, spglib and pymatgen==2024.4.13. The latter loads held-out group labels solely for post-generation stratification.

The confirm.py experiment freezes the same method and seed-11 checkpoint, evaluates five draws on 384 official test targets sorted by the SHA-256 of the prefix crytaldrift-onecall-transfer-confirm: followed by the material ID, and compares to released uFlowCSP and the original three angular-A fits on those exact IDs at those draw budgets. It writes generated structures and all discrete donor choices before accessing target geometries. Invalid primitive conversions count as unsuccessful attempts. See confirmation/summary.json. These subset results do not replace the full 9,046-target scores.

After inspecting the five-draw result, extend.py retains the exact first five structures and adds candidate positions 6–20 in the same donor ranking with an independent suffix random stream. An exploratory native-volume sensitivity rescales the refined cell isotropically to the sampled symbolic seed volume without a second neural call. The scale-enabled matcher is re-evaluated on every available prefix pair and a deterministic subset of suffix pairs to check its behavior numerically. Outputs are under confirmation/extension.
