# Validation-only cell-strain sensitivity

The generator's bounded dyads allow its log aspect ratio to change by at most 0.25 per draw. On 2,048 random training records, 1,276 empirical-prior seeds fall outside that necessary native-representation condition. Multiply only the traceless log strain by four, preserving the isotropic change, site coordinates, parameter count and initialization. Train seed 11 using the exact same exposures, positive banks, random seeds, optimizer and validation noises as the primary angular arm. Evaluate after 16, 64 and 256 updates on the existing 128 validation IDs with five proposals each. Compare paired per-ID fair energy scores to their matched angular checkpoints. The decision at 256 updates uses a two-sided 95% paired bootstrap interval with 5,000 resamples. Only consider advancing the wider head if its upper difference bound is below zero. No official test target enters this pilot. Timing, conditioning and any stronger follow-up are identified separately.
