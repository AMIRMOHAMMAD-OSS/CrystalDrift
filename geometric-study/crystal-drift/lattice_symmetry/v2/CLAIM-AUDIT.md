## Quantitative verdict

| Question | Result | Identified evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Does the finite training computation respect independent crystal representations? | In the nine-crystal FP64 test the largest gradient change was 5.29e-14. Restoring all nine validation-selected physical fits and testing their actual eight-block objective gave at most 4.03e-14 across tested outputs. | `results/physics-acceptance.json`, `results/checkpoint-symmetry-selected.json` |
| Do center powers and directional harmonics help raw CSP? | At twenty draws, angular 31.79%, center-only 31.47%, pair shells 31.30%, fitted seed 26.73%. Angular minus radial was +0.49 points [0.38, 0.60]. The same angular field with a fixed-basis backbone reached 30.27%. The earlier fixed-basis drift and released uFlowCSP reached 32.91% and 79.32%. | `csp/quality.csv`, `csp/paired-draws.csv`, `csp/seeds.csv` |
| Do actual clocks or native cell volume alter that ranking? | At 100 ms the balanced stream recovered 32/128 angular, 36 radial, 39 prior, 38 earlier fixed-basis and 13 matched flow. Angular minus matched flow was +14.84 points [8.59, 21.09]. Qualifying twenty-draw recovery by native volume gave angular 21.24% and radial 24.60%, a paired −3.37 points [−3.74, −2.98]. Single-thread CPU B1 was 4.57 ms for angular and 17.14 ms for matched flow16. | `csp/interleaved/{recovery,paired}.csv`, `csp/geometry-sensitivity.csv`, `csp/cpu/timing.csv` |
| What improves in multielement generation? | Among 10,000 shared compositions, angular vs radial structural validity was 95.59% vs 94.29%. The paired difference was +1.30 points [1.08, 1.53]. Coverage recall changed by −0.36 points [−0.83, +0.08]. No strict physical hit in the first sixteen of either arm. Angular had six converged relaxations and radial had eight. | `denovo/verified.json`, `denovo/per-draw-verification.json`, `denovo/physical-results.csv` |
| What does the supplied template buy? | For one draw on all 9,046 targets, C angular matched 4,549 versus 4,152 for the same untrained template seed, a gain of +4.39 points [3.90, 4.90]. On 128 separate templates, 20 draws gave 106 C-angular matches and 121 for the 40-call DiffCSP++ checkpoint. At 0.01 Å, 2,556/2,560 C-angular draws and 15/16 relaxed cases had exactly the requested operation set. | `wyckoff/full/summary.json`, `wyckoff/summary.json`, `wyckoff/operation-order-audit.json` |

The eight vector figures and all 21 manuscript pages were inspected after clean compilation. The [clean-build receipt](release/clean-build.json) records 23 source members, 34 citations, 36 linked reference targets and 25 embedded fonts. References start on page 14 and the mathematical and experimental supplement starts on page 16.

The key quantitative results are recorded by material ID or draw ID in `audit.json`. Every performance conclusion conditions on the stated sampler, data, head, optimizer-selected checkpoint, number of draws and hardware. A validity predicate, an approximate hull screen and experimental synthesizability measure different things. No experiment in this version certifies a general thermodynamic or directional-harmonic advantage.
