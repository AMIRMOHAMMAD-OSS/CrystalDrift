## Archive and reproduction checks

I reran the binary, WBM, ablation, training and expanded manuscript verifiers in read-only mode, intercepting report writes and comparing their outputs with the sealed files. These checks recovered the original summaries for 2,713,800 compact conditional predictions, the 180,920 conditional CrystalFlow predictions, seven GPU timing workloads, 60,000 de novo draws, the physical screens and DFT records. I separately reran the Fourier witness and the exact finite-bank expectation enumeration. The research ZIP includes every regular data or code file opened by the three record-level verifiers during this check.

All six hashes in the fixed release manifest matched. CRC checks passed on every member of its five ZIP files, comprising 38 arXiv source members, 14,163 research members, 13 editable-figure members, 293 crystal-package members and 42 benchmark-table members. The 38 extracted source files also matched their individual original hashes. Two sequential PDFLaTeX passes from a clean extraction, with shell escape and package installation disabled, produced 38 pages with no unresolved references, LaTeX warnings or overfull boxes. At 72 dpi the rebuilt and sealed PDFs rendered pixel-identically on all pages. The SHA-256 of the concatenated PPM render was `4134df99fab6f4fd370279338126f847592efdef66b62f9de5fcbf00d38e6698`. The mathematical supplement starts on page 22, after the references on page 20.

The fixed paper meets the pasted request's two-task scope as a proposal-quality and compute study. Its speed advantage depends on the comparator. Stronger one-step and retrieval controls win the brief deadline comparison, and the learned-prior drifting geometry covers fewer references than iterative flow. Lang2Str and MatterGen have different inputs or evaluation criteria and appear as published context. The local iterative and joint crystal comparisons use released CrystalFlow. The fixed release ran on the available local CPU and RTX 3060 Laptop GPU.
