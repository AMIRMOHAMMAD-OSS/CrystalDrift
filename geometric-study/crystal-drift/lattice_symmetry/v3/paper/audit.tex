\section{Experimental conventions and diagnostic checks}\label{app:benchmark-conventions}
\paragraph{Recovery.} For target $i$ and ordered candidate $j$, let $r_{ij}$ be the normalized RMS distance returned by \texttt{StructureMatcher.get\_rms\_dist(proposal,target)}. Assign $r_{ij}=+\infty$ to a missing or rejected proposal. The reported rate is $N^{-1}\sum_i\mathbf{1}[\min_{j\leq k}r_{ij}<\infty]$. Average the minimum RMS over recovered targets only. When two methods recover different sets, compute an additional paired displacement summary on their intersection. The public pymatgen matcher \cite{pymatgen} uses a primitive cell, species-preserving comparison, length tolerance 0.3, site tolerance 0.5 and angle tolerance $10^\circ$, with uniform volume rescaling. Supercell and subset matching are disabled. A native-volume success requires a matching candidate with $|\log(V_{ij}/V_i)|\leq\log(1.1)$ on the same draw. The maximum-site criterion uses the public fit decision. The audit samples direct public comparisons alongside a certified necessary-condition screen.

For an alignment of $n$ sites, let $d_a$ be the Cartesian displacement after periodic assignment and subtraction of the common translation. With $\bar V$ the volume of the matcher's average lattice, the RMS is $(n^{-1}\sum_a\|d_a\|^2)^{1/2}/(\bar V/n)^{1/3}$. The result is dimensionless. The maximum-site sensitivity applies the same length normalization to the largest site displacement using the public fit search.

\paragraph{Volume sensitivity.} For each learned proposal, rescale its lattice by $(V_{\rm seed}/V_{\rm out})^{1/3}$. The seed volume is available during generation. Uniform scaling leaves the scale-enabled match unchanged in exact arithmetic. On all 480 ordered development proposals from the frozen small angular model we recomputed both public comparisons. All match indicators agreed and the maximum absolute normalized RMS difference was $3.9\times10^{-16}$. On these 96 targets the number satisfying the same-draw native-volume condition rose from 44 to 52.

\paragraph{Training geometry and field response.} The orbit sampler has 26,419 eligible native primitive training records. For 22,916 an exact-formula reference bank contains one distinct structure. The first positive is the scheduled anchor, the second a draw with replacement from its exact composition. Independent seed draws supply two queries and two negatives. The within-minibatch affinity normalizations can consequently involve duplicate columns. Both distances use the same calibrated temperatures, 0.7419 and 1.9785, derived from 256 registered training cases. Pair, center-order-zero and full angular features retain the same scaling of shared blocks. Of the 105,676 precomputed seed draws in the first traversal, 544 fall back to $P1$, and 86.44\% have a movable coordinate in at least one occupied orbit. The training-bank audits record the positive and negative effective sample sizes and feature and parameter gradient norms. A 24-update real-data overfit check attained nonzero lattice motion (up to 1.09~\AA{} in Frobenius norm) and site motion (up to 0.31~\AA).

\paragraph{Finite checks on the geometric statements.} Independent changes of basis, origin, wrapping, site order, repeated-orbit labels and Cartesian frame act on queries and each bank member. Test fixtures include triclinic shear, improper frames, glide and screw operations, coincident and empty environments, fixed orbits and lattice/image cutoffs. A raw lattice comparison detects a spurious distance and a fixed Miller-index box loses physical frequencies in the shear control. On the restored 218,497-parameter angular checkpoint the nine Hall-group checks in double precision gave a maximum cell discrepancy of $1.42\times10^{-14}$~\AA{} and a subgroup motif discrepancy below $1.4\times10^{-15}$~\AA. The canonical-chart MeanFlow objective has a separate finite-difference directional derivative check. That loss contains endpoint assignment, component-wise torus wraps and a lattice-matrix error in a chosen chart. The invariance proof for the physical drifting loss does not imply independent-representation invariance for that training coupling.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and exposure.} Confidence intervals resample complete targets, retaining every candidate and all fitted seeds of that target. The family challenge resamples the 24 held-out family clusters. The intervals condition on trained weights; differences across independently initialized fits are also reported. Overlap of two marginal intervals is insufficient evidence for equality. Complete passes and structure/atom exposure appear separately from the number of neural calls during generation. The empirical composition prior for de novo sampling has zero mass on reduced formulas outside its training catalog. Structural novelty there can arise through an arrangement at a known composition.

\paragraph{Validity, coverage and distributions in the de novo pools.} Each pipeline receives the same sequence of 10,000 unit-cell compositions. A raw cell passes structural validity if its volume is at least \(0.1\,\text{\AA}^3\) and every distinct site pair is separated by at least \(0.5\,\text{\AA}\) at its nearest periodic image. This test omits same-site images. For compositional validity, the SMACT screen accepts single elements and all-metal compositions, or requires an oxidation-state assignment with zero total charge and compatible Pauling electronegativity \cite{smact}. The implementation accepts a charge-neutral assignment when the electronegativity test lacks data and rejects searches exceeding \(10^7\) charge combinations. Joint validity requires both criteria on one draw. Within each reduced formula, the first as-generated representative of each group under the public maximum-site fit test counts as unique. A candidate counts as novel if the same test finds no species-preserving match among eligible training structures. All membership decisions retain the ordered attempts and their errors.

For geometric coverage we average 61 CrystalNN features over sites and standardize 132 Magpie compositional features with the released benchmark constants \cite{cdvae,diffcsp,matminer}. In each direction, compute the two nearest Euclidean distances in their respective feature spaces. Coverage accepts a case if its structural distance is at most 0.4 and its compositional distance is at most 10. Different neighbors may supply the two minima. Recall divides the number of covered reference fingerprints by 9,046. Precision divides the number of covered proposal fingerprints by all 10,000 attempts. A seeded sample of 1,000 jointly valid generated cells with available fingerprints, without replacement in each pool, supplies the density and distinct-element-count Wasserstein estimates against the full test distribution. Because the raw formula streams are identical, differences in the latter distance reflect eligibility and subsampling.

\paragraph{Physical screen.} A hash selected eight of the 10,000 unconditional draw indices before their predicted energies were inspected. Each candidate and competing phase uses the same pretrained CHGNet~0.3.0 potential \cite{chgnet}, zero-pressure FIRE optimizer with cell and position degrees of freedom, 100-step cap, and generalized force threshold 0.10~eV/\AA. CHGNet was pretrained on Materials Project trajectories. This surrogate can share materials with the evaluation set. The finite MP-20 competing-phase library takes the lowest deposited formation energy per available reduced composition among phases with deposited hull distance at most 0.10~eV/atom, together with elemental terminals. A complete restricted-hull label requires all competitor relaxations and elemental references. The acceptance gate requires convergence, contact distances at least 0.60 of the pairwise sums of covalent radii, an energy within 0.10~eV/atom of the candidate-augmented restricted hull, novelty against original MP-20 training and uniqueness within the relaxed panel. A zero-success panel of eight admits success probabilities up to $1-0.05^{1/8}=31.2\%$ at a one-sided 95\% level under independent Bernoulli sampling. This small surrogate screen cannot establish DFT stability or experimental synthesizability.
