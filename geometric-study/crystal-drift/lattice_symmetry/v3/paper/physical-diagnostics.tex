For the cell-shape diagnostic, Niggli-reduce the lattice, form its Gram matrix and divide by the cube root of its determinant. The error is the Euclidean distance between the sorted log eigenvalues of the proposed and reference unit-determinant matrices. This spectral statistic can coincide for different packings. For site motion we keep the paired seed's site ordering, wrap each fractional component of the update into the centered unit interval and convert to Cartesian displacement in the seed lattice. For a skew cell this chart convention can choose a different image from the shortest physical displacement. The site-median column takes the median within each draw, then the median across draws.

On these same 1,920 ordered proposals, the median log-volume change from the paired seed was +0.069 for 2M contact drift and -0.318 for same-backbone MeanFlow. Their median site-motion summaries were 0.293 and 0.293~\AA, respectively. These paired changes describe the network's action even for target proposals which never match.
