One neural evaluation can predict a periodic cell and all its sites, but a structural prior can recover targets before the network is called. We adapt training-time attraction and repulsion to a formula-conditioned generator that jointly predicts a lattice and its occupied sites. Its periodic angular field admits two separately tested additions, typed inter-orbit contacts and reciprocal-space intensities. On the 9,046-target MP-20 test split, five proposals recovered 53.01\% from the symbolic prior and 57.19\% after a contact-trained 2M-parameter update, a gain of +4.18 percentage points (95\% interval +3.69 to +4.67). The angular update reached 57.16\%, same-backbone MeanFlow 56.90\%, released uFlowCSP 68.05\% and direct training-geometry retrieval 86.07\%. At 100 ms per formula on a single GPU, the contact update recovered 34/64 targets, released uFlowCSP 37/64, and zero-call retrieval of training geometries 55/64. With complete prototype families withheld, the seed-11 small contact update recovered 49/228 against 46/228 for its prior. Contact and ordinary angular drift give nearly identical recovery. Restoring starting volumes to the contact model raises native-volume-qualified hits from 3,481 to 4,296, above 3,970 for the symbolic prior.
