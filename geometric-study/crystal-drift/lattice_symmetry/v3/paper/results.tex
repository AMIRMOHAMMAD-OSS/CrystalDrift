\section{Unconditional sampling at training compositions}

We draw an exact native-cell formula uniformly over retained training records, then draw its crystal. This empirical law preserves the observed frequency of each composition. It assigns zero mass to unknown compositions. Every method receives the same 10,000 sampled formulas in the same order. The materials question is whether a proposed arrangement meets structural and chemical criteria at those compositions.
