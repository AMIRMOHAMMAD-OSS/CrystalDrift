\section{Template coverage and independent structural checks}\label{app:occupancy-coverage}

For every target, reproduce the protected training lookup using only its formula. At each
ordered draw record the inferred group and the full element-labelled list of occupied
Wyckoff letters. A literal match uses the canonical Hall setting. To recognize equivalent
occupations under alternative settings, apply one simultaneous Wyckoff-letter permutation
to the entire multiset of orbits. The tabulated alternative settings in PyXtal~1.0.9 \cite{pyxtal} supply
878 distinct permutations across the 230 groups. Each group's list contains identity and is
closed under composition. A recovered space-group number alone gives a less restrictive
diagnostic. In 7 of 9,046 official structures the reference chart failed verification. These
targets remain in every coverage denominator. At five draws 6,998 targets have the literal
occupation on the shortlist, 7,469 have a setting-equivalent occupation and 7,705 have the
reference group. Among the 45,230 proposed templates at this prefix, 6,331 have zero free
positional parameters. The network can still change their compatible cell.

The original shared-family panel holds out 96 chemical compositions in 96 represented
families. To ask whether any of their geometries are unfamiliar at the matcher's resolution,
compare each native challenge structure in that panel (drawn from the protected part of the
official training partition) to the surviving training donors in its family under an anonymous
species bijection. Pymatgen's \texttt{get\_rms\_anonymous} uses the same geometric tolerances
and volume scaling as the main analysis. With donors ordered by a fixed hash, the search
finds a witness for every target in 114 comparisons. This panel therefore measures chemical
transfer within represented geometries at these tolerances.

We subsequently registered a bounded validation search before applying any generator to its
selected targets. Exclude 2,039 records identified in previous validation panels. For a
verified primitive target with a free positional coordinate, require one to 64 training
structures with the same group and anonymous species partition of occupied Wyckoff letters
up to a simultaneous setting change. Rank the eligible 3,734 validation IDs by SHA-256 with
a fixed salt and inspect the first 512. A witness establishes coverage. An uncovered case
requires exhausting its entire compatible catalog. With 864 public matcher calls, 487 cases
have a witness and 25 (25 distinct patterns) have none. Freeze the 25 uncovered cases and the
first 96 covered controls before scoring their generated proposals. This definition concerns
matches within the recorded occupancy catalog. Other symbolic patterns and other matcher
tolerances can admit further geometric analogues.

\paragraph{Numerical operation checks.}
Primitive-cell reduction of all 1,731 conventional Wyckoff charts gives the expected
multiplicities and free ranks. At two parameter choices per chart the largest bidirectional
wrapped-coordinate residual against both the conventional images and complete native group
orbit is \(8.9\times10^{-16}\). The native operation lists also agree with independently
read spglib~2.5.0 Hall operations for every group \cite{spglib}. On 32 further hash-selected validation formulas,
the zero-call prior and four frozen learned methods each generated one structure. At
\(0.01\,\text{\AA}\) all 160 proposals contain the requested operations, have exactly that
operation set, and return the same canonical Hall number and equivalent occupied pattern through
PyXtal's public \texttt{from\_seed}. PyXtal assigns the canonical setting for the detected
space-group type. The independent in-basis operation check establishes containment.
Its \texttt{to\_pymatgen} returns conventional structures.
The stock scale-enabled matcher accepts 149 of 160 native-to-conventional round trips in
both directions. Independently reducing each member of the remaining 11 pairs with spglib
recovers all 11 in both directions. This separate convention check leaves the headline
proposal-first matching protocol unchanged.
