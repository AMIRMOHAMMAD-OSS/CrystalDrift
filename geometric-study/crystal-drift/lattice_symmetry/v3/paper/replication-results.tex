\section{Training-seed variability and the native priors}

Each model in the replication experiment traverses its complete eligible training set. Development MR@5 selects between the half- and full-traversal checkpoints independently in each fit. For each target the three fitted weights use the same ordered initial draws. We first average paired win-minus-loss indicators over the three weights for that target, then resample targets or protected families. The interval describes target sampling for these three fitted models. The deposited per-seed results retain variation among the fits.

Matching the training seed within each contact--angular pair gives a five-draw difference of +0.00 percentage points (95\% interval -0.07 to +0.08) at 0.2M parameters and +0.01 percentage points (95\% interval -0.03 to +0.04) at 2M. Both contrasts share the starting proposals and target IDs.

For 2M contact drift, scale-enabled five-draw hit counts across fits were 5,173, 5,172, 5,170. The corresponding native-volume counts were 3,481, 3,631, 3,137, versus 3,970 for the common prior. Agreement of scale-enabled rates across independent training runs obscures much larger variation in native-volume recovery.
