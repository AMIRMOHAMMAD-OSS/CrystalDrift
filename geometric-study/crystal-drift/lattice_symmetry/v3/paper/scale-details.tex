A direct small-model replay exposed a boundary of the matcher. For material \texttt{mp-8584}, draw 2, the public matcher accepted the archived CUDA coordinates with normalized RMS displacement 0.445342 and returned no match for a regenerated CPU realization. The maximum perturbations over the 480 development proposals were $3.82\times10^{-6}$~\AA{} in the cell and $1.20\times10^{-7}$ in fractional coordinates. One flag changed and all native-volume-qualified target counts agreed. A CUDA replay using a cached-input shortcut also changed this decision. The production validation path retained its original seed construction and its archived arrays. The release identifies the device on which each output was produced.

The first orbit MeanFlow experiment used the same anchor set as drift but also drew event times from the within-shard shuffle generator. Only 328 of 26,419 ordered positions agreed with angular drift. The strict control draws times from a separate seed stream. Its complete ordered anchor sequence agrees with drift at every position. This distinction affects comparisons of training trajectories even when total exposure and distinct IDs agree.

\paragraph{Earlier datasets and controls.} The preceding fixed-basis study includes a 128-target binary MP-20 cohort, a separate full-test replay, Wang--Botti--Marques structures from the Matbench Discovery release \cite{wbm,matbench}, and an Alexandria allocation from the Alex-MP-20 release \cite{mattergen} with reduced-formula exclusions. For WBM, a new reduced-formula split of 256,259 relaxed cells gives 205,091 training, 25,117 validation and 26,051 test entries. Seeds 11, 23 and 37 were evaluated on the archived 128-target WBM test cohort. Those archived experiments use the models and splits of that release. The periodic models developed here have new protected donors and primitive-orbit eligibility. Their official MP-20 denominator is 9,046. The source bundle identifies the checksums and independent reconstruction commands for both earlier releases.

\paragraph{Training-only crystal-system label.} In the small angular control an auxiliary linear head predicts one of seven crystal systems from the multiplicity-weighted mean orbit embedding. We add $0.1$ times the cross-entropy for the two active generated draws with the labels of their registered same-formula positive partners. The $7\times 114$ weights and seven biases add 805 training-only parameters. Generation discards the classifier and receives the same formula and symbolic seed as ordinary angular drift.

On the 128 WBM targets, five draws recovered 39/40/37 targets for fixed-basis drift, 28/35/27 for one Euler step, 47/47/39 for 16 Euler steps and 113/115/115 for training retrieval (seeds 11/23/37). The drift-minus-16-step difference was -4.43 percentage points (95\% interval -8.59 to 0.00), averaging the three paired outcomes per target before resampling targets.
