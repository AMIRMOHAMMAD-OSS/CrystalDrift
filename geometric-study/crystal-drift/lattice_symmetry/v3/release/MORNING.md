# CrytalDrift author review

**What survives the prior.** On all 9,046 public MP-20 compositions, the selected 2M contact model recovered 5,173/9,046 (57.19%) at five draws. Returning the identical symbolic starts recovered 4,795/9,046 (53.01%). There were 445 gains and 67 losses, a net change of +4.18 pp [+3.69, +4.67]. Ordinary angular drift recovered 5,171/9,046 (57.16%) and same-backbone MeanFlow recovered 5,147/9,046 (56.90%). Adding contacts to angular drift changed recovery by +0.02 pp [-0.04, +0.10]. The new descriptor supplies a verifiable invariant construction. This comparison finds little practical extra recovery from its contact channels.

**Capacity and transfer of coordinates.** The 136M contact fit matched the 2M fit at 5,173 recoveries. Requiring native-volume agreement reduced the 136M count to 1,648. The highest five-draw count across frozen orbit and graph fits using symbolic starts was 5,202/9,046 (sitegraph-small). Direct transfer of complete training geometries recovered 7,786/9,046 (86.07%). Applying an angular network to those coordinates recovered 7,780/9,046 (86.00%).

**Volume and larger cells.** Requiring agreement within the same multiplicative factor of 1.10 on physical volume gives 3,481 contact hits against 3,970 prior hits, a change of -5.41 pp [-6.25, -4.53]. On 5,109 cells with at least nine sites, scale-enabled MR@5 changed by +5.66 pp [+5.01, +6.34]. Conditional displacement and the separate maximum-site decision are in MAIN-COMPARISON.csv. Restoring each starting proposal's own volume while keeping updated sites and shape recovered 4,296 native hits.

**Controls and time.** Released uFlowCSP recovered 6,156/9,046 (68.05%) at five draws. The contact-minus-released gap was -10.87 pp [-11.92, -9.77]. On the 64-target interleaved RTX 3060 Laptop clock at 100 ms per target, contacts completed 720 proposals and recovered 34 targets. Released uFlowCSP completed 305 and recovered 37, a paired difference of -4.69 pp [-17.19, +7.81]. Retrieval of full training geometries recovered 55/64. CPU batch-one medians were 5.64 and 280.86 ms for contacts and released uFlowCSP. The corresponding GPU 10,000-proposal workloads took 17.87 and 36.81 s. The clock includes lookup and host-side output.

**Training and transfer.** The 2,098,297-parameter contact fit processed 211,352 training anchors over eight traversals of 26,419 retained structures. Validation selected its 13,184-anchor checkpoint. At five draws the small contact model recovered 49/228 (21.49%) on 24 withheld families against 46/228 (20.18%) (+1.32 pp [+0.00, +3.23]). None of those families had its exact occupied pattern in the donor bank. Shared families with held-out chemistries gave +10.42 pp [+4.17, +16.67]. The exploratory previously unmatched arrangements gave 1/25 (4.00%) and the same result for their prior. At 20 draws the small contact fits recovered 88/88/88 versus 69 for the prior (+8.33 pp [+4.61, +12.90]).

**Generated structures.** Seven methods each produced 10,000 candidates at the same sampled training compositions. The valid, unique and novel counts were 4,140 for the symbolic prior, 4,365 for the 2M contact update and 4,505 for released uFlowCSP. On eight prospectively selected draws, the contact model had 0/8 joint successes under the restricted CHGNet screen. The illustrated threshold-passing SrPbS2 relaxes to a training donor. The screen measures surrogate relaxation against a finite phase library.

**Author decision.** The paired improvement over the symbolic seed, its loss on physical volume, and the weak transfer to unrepresented families jointly support a paper on what a one-call periodic update learns. Direct geometry retrieval and the restricted physical screen constrain the discovery claim. Validating discrete occupations and native cell volume on unseen families is a more discriminating next experiment than another descriptor on the same starting distribution.
