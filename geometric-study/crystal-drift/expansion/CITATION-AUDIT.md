# Citation and source check

All 30 linked bibliography titles and their first authors were reconciled with publisher-supplied Crossref records, official conference proceedings or arXiv citation metadata. The dated records, source endpoints and title comparisons are in [citation-metadata.json](citation-metadata.json). The script traverses the files included by the manuscript, checks every citation key and reports any bibliography entries awaiting a citation. Run `python expansion/citation_verify.py` from the repository root. NeurIPS lists *All that structure matches does not glitter* in its 2025 proceedings. Its individual metadata record has a 2026 electronic publication date.

The role of the remaining citations is specific. The original pymatgen, matminer and SMACT papers identify the software and chemistry tests. Gneiting and Raftery provide the multivariate energy score. Grimm and Baake and Mandereau et al. discuss homometric structures and cyclic Z-relations. Kernel-Gradient Drifting is a related field construction. The CHGNet, Quantum ESPRESSO and PSLibrary references identify the potential, plane-wave calculation and pseudopotential source. The finite-bank result and numerical witness remain CrytalDrift calculations and are audited against their own code.

On 20 November 2026, targeted arXiv searches combined “drifting”, “attraction repulsion”, “crystal generation”, “lattice”, “fractional coordinates” and “crystal structure prediction”. The relevant primary records establish prior one-step crystal generation and prior symmetry-aware molecular drifting. We worded the contribution as a concrete adaptation of the training-time field to a composition-conditioned periodic crystal generator. The manuscript makes no universal priority claim.
