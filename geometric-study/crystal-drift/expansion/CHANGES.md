# Changes for the expanded preprint

- Reframed the paper around two questions. How often can one network call recover a supplied-composition structure within the time budget, and what geometry does it generate when a fitted prior supplies the composition? The opening figure compares actual first-hit times and completed draws on the same GPU cohort.
- Added a dedicated compact MeanFlow control and an independently sampled positive-bank arm. Twelve three-seed training trajectories supply thirty-six checkpoints at one, three and ten passes. All training-duration choices use validation. The original one-Euler diagnostic and pair-feature ablation remain available.
- Extended the conditional comparison to the official MP-20 split and released iterative weights. Added native-volume rematching, separate volume/shape/site errors and an independent, formula-audited 512-target hybrid test. Preserved the earlier binary, WBM and Alexandria evidence.
- Added five ordered, unfiltered 10,000-structure de novo banks. The analysis compares shared sampled compositions and a released joint generator, covers all seven DiffCSP raw metrics, and reports uniqueness, novelty, learned-prior support and matched GPU timings.
- Screened the first sixteen candidates per pipeline against the same CHGNet relaxation protocol and a documented phase library containing 671 distinct competing phases. Added actual raw and relaxed CIFs, a failed example, reference costs and a preselected fixed-composition first-principles check.
- Extended the mathematical supplement with the conditional all-independent-positive proof, the bounded-continuous weak-limit theorem, its numerical check and the factorization of the de novo law. The finite descriptor collision, basis dependence and the adverse or null feature outcomes remain explicit.
- Preserved the author's byline, affiliation and spelling. The release includes an editable method diagram and timing/training figures, a compact benchmark-table download and a section-by-section claim audit. The review package stops before arXiv submission.

- Replayed the released joint 100-step CrystalFlow model locally. Generating its 10,000 draws took 29.33 minutes on the RTX 3060 Laptop GPU. Its accuracy evaluation and separately registered sixteen-draw screen use those same structures. A four-setting conditional replay also isolates the effect of five versus 100 integration steps from site-velocity annealing.

## Scientific verdict for author review

The evidence supports a methods-and-benchmark preprint on the adaptation of the training-time drifting objective to periodic joint lattice/site proposals. Its strongest computational claim is a measured recovery advantage over the iterative compact control under short serial deadlines. Dedicated one-step models and retrieval are strong controls. Its strongest theoretical claim is a finite, independent-bank weak-limit result for the implemented feature field, accompanied by an explicit collision that limits structural identification. The learned-prior experiment demonstrates complete multielement generation and quantifies the coverage it loses. The limited CHGNet phase library and a targeted MgGa3 first-principles example support a physical screening assessment. They leave a general thermodynamic discovery advantage unresolved. All rates and intervals apply to their stated designs, hardware, archives and trained seeds.
