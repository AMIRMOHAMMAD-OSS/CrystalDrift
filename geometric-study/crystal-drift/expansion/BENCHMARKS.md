# Benchmarks and comparability

We checked the named original articles and the public release material available on 20 November 2026. A supplementary search used “crystal structure generation drifting models attraction repulsion,” “periodic crystal lattice one-step drifting,” and both spellings of the model name. It did not establish exclusive priority. We describe the specific adaptation and show its operating regime. uFlowCSP, OMatG-flash and geometric drifting methods already establish the broader one-step and symmetry-aware precedents. For the initially suggested autoregressive comparators see [CrystaLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04340) and [Wyckoff Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02407).

The conditional experiment fixes the per-cell species multiset for each ID. The de novo experiment supplies a random source and its fitted chemistry prior, or lets a joint model generate its own atoms. Published values in the paper serve as context and have their original denominators. The local tables use retained predictions and name the exact cohort.

| Model | Inputs and source | Evaluation and cost boundary |
|---|---|---|
| DiffCSP | MP-20 CSP uses a supplied species vector. The de novo variant adds element generation. | Raw per-target StructureMatcher matching, and generation metrics over the 10,000-candidate pool. The released proxy evaluation samples 1,000 valid proposals and uses the released crystal property network. Our local five-arm de novo evaluation specifies its own checks of this implementation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.04475) · [Code and released evaluator](https://github.com/jiaor17/DiffCSP) |
| FlowMM | Periodic flow matching on atoms, sites and the lattice. CSP fixes atoms; unconditional generation uses atom-type analog bits. MP-20 and MPTS-52 are distinct tasks. | Official scripts distinguish reconstruction, raw generation metrics, relaxation and a dated MP hull. Its training and GPU integration count belong to its own protocol. [Paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/miller24a.html) · [Evaluation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/flowmm) |
| CrystalFlow | Continuous cell/site flow conditional on composition, or joint generation that generates atom types. | We import the authors' 10,000 unrelaxed POSCAR entries in deposited order and keep the empty entry. The conditional replay uses the released MP-20 checkpoint with the integration count written on every row. The published 100-step line belongs to the original operating point. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64364-4) · [Deposited predictions](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29978608.v3) |
| uFlowCSP | Released Cartesian MP-20 EMA weights, ordered species, null symmetry input, 100 padded tokens. Its own training covers the broader public training set including the chemistry unavailable to the small binary fits. | Retrospective raw matching of 1, 5 and 20 owned proposals on all 9,046 IDs. External published times at batch 20 are distinct from the local synchronized GPU timing, local CPU timing, and de novo sampling with the shared learned prior. [Paper and Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09799v1) · [Code](https://github.com/usccolumbia/uFlowCSP) |
| Lang2Str | An LLM samples a composition; CSPML predicts the space group; an LLM description and a conditioned flow determine geometry. Its oracle receives extra information. | The paper reports 10,000 de novo outputs and separate proxy, CHGNet and DFT evaluations. Its Table 4 gives distinct ordinary and oracle CSP rows. A standalone author-released evaluator and prediction pool could not be verified for a same-hardware replay. [Pipeline and tables](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03946v1) |
| MatterGen | The model jointly samples atoms, lattice and sites. Its model card describes up to twenty atoms and a filtered MP-plus-Alexandria training chemistry. Optional property conditioning changes the task. | The published unconditional panel uses 1,024 draws and a disorder-aware definition of novelty, with energy referenced to a competing-phase hull. The release distinguishes supplied DFT energies from relaxed surrogate estimates and provides energy correction options. Its SUN rate has a different reference and relaxation boundary from ours. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08628-5) · [Model card](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/mattergen) · [Release and evaluation CLI](https://github.com/microsoft/mattergen) |
| Deng et al., SymDrift and TokenDrift | Image features, symmetry-aware molecular conformers, and text embeddings, respectively. | Image quality is reported with NFE, conformers with coverage, displacement and latency, and text with perplexity and entropy at 4/8/16 NFEs. These works inform the assessment of speed and quality. Their task-specific scores have no direct crystal equivalent. [Deng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04770) · [SymDrift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06140) · [TokenDrift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19470) |
| Compact MeanFlow | Same composition, periodic sites, width and family of cell networks. An interval-conditioned velocity is trained directly for a full source-to-target update. | Count one forward evaluation at generation. A forward-mode JVP enters training; training hours and examples are reported separately. [Original MeanFlow identity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13447) |

All raw local CSP rows use primitive-cell reduction, isotropic volume rescaling, exact species, no subset/supercell search, lattice tolerance 0.3, site tolerance 0.5 and angle tolerance 10°. A target contributes its lowest successful normalized displacement within its own ordered prefix. The maximum-site and native-volume checks use the same output structures. Training templates are retrieved exclusively from the associated training split. Earlier inspections of the official MP-20 test and original Alexandria transfer set are documented in the manuscript. The separate 512-formula cohort was chosen under the frozen hybrid protocol.

For CrystalFlow, a second full de novo run uses the official 100-step joint sampler, its released atom-count law and fresh random noise on the common GPU. Its measured generation time, raw quality and consecutive relaxed structures refer to that same local draw sequence. The conditional four-setting check varies five versus 100 steps and site-velocity annealing at fixed noise for the 128-target prefix. [Native sampler and commands](external/README.md)
